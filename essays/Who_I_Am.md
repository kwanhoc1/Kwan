## What Defines Me
I had one philosophical thought recently. It is a fundamental question. 'Who am I? What makes me "myself"?' Do my past deeds define who I am? Will my future deeds defind who I will be? Or is my current presence in reality the only definition of me? How about how other people perceive me? Do countless versions of me in other people's mind define who I am? And there is a version of me that I think of myself. Who am I? This is a question that I have to be asking myself forever, and I found some answers from my experience in ICS 414.

## 
ICS 414 is a project based software engineering class. It was designed to give us the experience of clienteling in tech industry. We were assigned to a team and we had to work on a semester long project. in the first week of the semester, I remember that only 3 out of 6 teammates showed up to the class. One of my teammate who showed up was worried and overwhelmed by the project and the situation we were in. Her attitude showed me that she's a person who is strongly responsible and is ready for the project. That was the first thing I learned from ICS 414 this semester. The attitude builds how people perceive you. So does my perception over my teammates define who they are?
